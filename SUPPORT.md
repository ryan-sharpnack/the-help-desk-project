# Getting Support

Welcome to The Help Desk Project!

Whether you're new to IT, building your first homelab, troubleshooting a technical problem, or contributing to the repository, we're here to help.

Our goal is to create a community where people can learn from one another, share technical knowledge, and develop practical IT skills.

## Where to Get Help

### 1. Technical Questions

Have a question about an IT concept, technology, configuration, or troubleshooting methodology?

Use GitHub Discussions to ask the community.

https://github.com/ryan-sharpnack/the-help-desk-project/discussions

### 2. Homelab Assistance

Need help configuring your homelab or troubleshooting an issue?

Share relevant details about your environment, the problem you're experiencing, and the steps you've already attempted.

The more information you provide, the easier it will be for others to help.

### 3. Career and Professional Development

Looking for advice about entering the IT industry, developing technical skills, or preparing for your first IT position?

Use GitHub Discussions to connect with other aspiring professionals and experienced industry members.

### 4. Repository Issues

Found an error in documentation, identified an issue with a contribution, or have an idea for improving the project?

Open a GitHub Issue.

Please use the appropriate issue template when one is available.

### 5. Mentorship

Experienced IT professionals interested in mentoring aspiring contributors are encouraged to participate in discussions, provide technical feedback, and share industry knowledge.

## Before Asking a Question

To help the community provide useful answers, please include:

- What you're trying to accomplish.
- Your operating system and relevant technologies.
- A description of the problem.
- Any error messages or relevant output.
- Troubleshooting steps you've already attempted.
- What you expected to happen versus what actually happened.

Never include passwords, API keys, private information, or confidential organizational data.

## Community Support

The Help Desk Project is a community-driven initiative.

We encourage everyone to help answer questions, share knowledge, and contribute to the development of others.

Please remember that contributors volunteer their time. Be respectful, patient, and appreciative of the assistance you receive.

## Important Disclaimer

Technical guidance shared by community members is provided for educational and informational purposes.

Always evaluate commands, scripts, configurations, and recommendations before implementing them in your environment.

Use isolated testing environments when experimenting with unfamiliar or potentially disruptive changes.

**Learn together. Troubleshoot together. Grow together.**
